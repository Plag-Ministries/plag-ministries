Look inside

A life much bigger than any one story.

From the sugarcane plantations of Hawaii to the Philippines at war, Bishop Granadosin’s path was built on survival and the weight of a divine calling. A young pastor initially rejected for his halting Tagalog. A Quonset hut church where the floorboards shook like a drum. The first Filipino ever elected “Bishop for Life,” and the only man to lead the United Methodist Church of the Philippines alone through the years of Martial Law.

Told in his own words — an account he called “a labor of love offered to you, the church, and God Almighty.”

A detail worth holding

The introduction carries Bishop Paul’s signature and the raised impression of his United Methodist Church episcopal dry seal.
