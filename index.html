Welcome to PLAG Ministries

A 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity dedicated to honoring faithful service, preserving community church history, and equipping the next generation of ministry leadership.

Many retired pastors, deaconesses, and church staff spend their lives serving local communities without access to structured pension plans or comprehensive retirement benefits. When unexpected health challenges or financial burdens arise in their later years, they often face these hardships in silence. At PLAG Ministries, we step into the gap by offering direct living assistance, medical grant relief, and ongoing pastoral care.

Our Roots & Inspiration

Inspired by the life and enduring faith of Bishop Paul Locke A. Granadosin and Socorro Granadosin.

From the sugarcane plantations of Hawaii to the Philippines at war, Bishop Granadosin’s path was built on survival and the weight of a divine calling — the first Filipino ever elected as a “Bishop for Life,” and the only man to lead the entire United Methodist Church of the Philippines alone through the years of Martial Law.

By capturing raw testimonies, preserving the history of Christian leadership, and extending practical care to those who walk in his footsteps, we honor his memory while building a lasting safety net for the servants who came after him.

Bishop in the Muddy Boots

A young pastor, initially rejected for his halting Tagalog, who found the heart of God in a Quonset hut church where the floorboards shook like a drum.
